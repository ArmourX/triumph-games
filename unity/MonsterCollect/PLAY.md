# How to Play — Monster Collect

Welcome, Trainer! This guide covers everything you need for your first session.

## Goal

Scan QR codes to collect monsters, raise them on your ranch, battle for rewards, breed new species, and share copies with friends.

## Getting Started

1. **Scan** — Open the Scan tab and tap **Scan**. Point your camera at any QR code.
2. **First monster** — A popup shows your new monster. It joins your ranch automatically.
3. **Ranch** — View all owned monsters. Tap one for details, training, breeding, or sharing.
4. **Battle** — Pick a fighter and challenge wild monsters for EXP and essence.
5. **Dex** — Track discovered species in your Monster Book (300 entries).

## Daily Limits

- **Energy** refills at midnight (scans, battles, and breeding spend energy).
- **Scans** have a daily cap — plan your hunting sessions.
- Everything works **offline**; online social features are optional.

## Tips

- Set an **active monster** on the ranch to use items and training.
- Open **Ranch Hub** for items, errantry trips, facilities, and decoration.
- Open **Goals** for quests, trainer rank, shop, and book rewards.
- Open **Social** for nearby Wi-Fi play, trades, and leaderboards.
- Share a monster QR so friends receive an exact copy (you keep yours).

## Settings

Tap the gear icon on any screen:

- Sound, music, haptics, and particles
- QR scan speed (faster = more battery use)
- **Accessibility** — text size, element symbols for colorblind support, reduced motion
- Replay tutorial or view **Credits**

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Try this |
|-------|----------|
| Camera won't start | Allow camera permission in system settings |
| "Ranch full" | Release a monster or upgrade ranch slots in Goals shop |
| "Out of energy" | Wait until midnight or manage daily actions |
| Unreadable QR | Hold steady, improve lighting, move closer |
| Invalid share code | Ask for a fresh share QR from the owner |

## Privacy & Data

Save data stays on your device at `{app storage}/monster_ranch.json`.  
Analytics and crash logs are stored locally for diagnostics during soft launch.
