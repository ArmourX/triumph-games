Class Guide

Overview of all classes, their roles, and recommended skill paths.

Warrior

Frontline tank with high HP and melee damage. Best for dungeon tanking and PvP bruiser builds.

Mage

Ranged AoE damage dealer. Excels in dungeon clears and burst PvP combos.

Rogue

High single-target DPS with stealth mechanics. Strong in arena and boss encounters.

Healer

Support class keeping parties alive. Essential for raid content and group dungeons.
