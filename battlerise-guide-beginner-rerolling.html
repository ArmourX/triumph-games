Beginner Tips for Rerolling

Best starter champions and how to build your first competitive team from your opening roll.

BattleRise · Triumph Guides · Last updated August 2026

What is rerolling?

When you start BattleRise, your account receives a random starter champion from the reroll pool — champions like Ursan, Etherstone Golem, Fionann, or Skeleton Knight are all possible outcomes.

If you don't like your roll, you can reroll to generate a new random champion before you commit to the account. This is your best chance to start with a high-impact unit before you've invested resources elsewhere.

Rerolling only affects this one starter pick — it does not reroll your entire roster. Once you accept a champion and progress, treat that choice as your anchor for early story, Arena, and your first artifact sets.

When should you stop rerolling?

Stop immediately if you roll Etherstone Golem — the only Mythic in the reroll pool and the best possible outcome.

Consider stopping if you roll a strong Rare that fills a role you need — especially a healer (Fionann) or a durable frontliner (Ursan, Skeleton Knight).

Keep rerolling if you roll Fara or Gozu and want a tank or healer first — both are fine damage dealers, but they won't carry early content alone. Don't chase the Golem forever; Ursan or Fionann is a solid account.

The reroll pool — every possible outcome

Only these six champions can appear on your opening roll. Champions like Invictus, Siegward, Bonelord, and Zephyr are not in this pool — you unlock those later through story, shards, and summons.

Mythic — stop rerolling

  • Etherstone Golem — STR support/tank; strong with invisibility comps

Rare — common reroll results

  • Ursan — versatile STR tank; assists on heals
  • Fionann — Verdant healer with revive
  • Skeleton Knight — armored frontline + debuffs
  • Fara — AGI debuffer
  • Gozu — execute-style STR burst

Building your first team around your roll

Every roster needs four roles filled as you unlock champions from summons and story:

  1. Frontline — absorbs damage (Ursan, Skeleton Knight, or Etherstone Golem from your roll)
  2. Main damage — clears waves (Fara or Gozu if you rolled them; otherwise your next summon)
  3. Support/healer — keeps the team alive (Fionann if you rolled her; otherwise priority summon)
  4. Control or secondary DPS — debuffs or burst from later champions you unlock

Your reroll champion should define which gap you don't need to fill first. Rolled Ursan or Etherstone Golem? Prioritize a healer and damage dealer from story rewards and shards. Rolled Fionann? Grab a tank and DPS next.

Quick tips for common reroll champions

Ursan (Rare STR)

One of the most common reroll results — and a good one. Ursan is a versatile tank that triggers assists when anyone heals. Pair him with Fionann or any healer, stack HP% and DEF% on STR artifacts, and he stabilizes your early Arena defense while you farm gear.

Etherstone Golem (Mythic STR)

A top-tier reroll jackpot. The Golem benefits from Invisibility and acts as flexible support — not a pure damage carry. Build tanky, protect him, and add AGI or INT damage dealers behind him. If you roll this, stop rerolling immediately.

Fionann (Rare INT)

Best Rare healer in the reroll pool. Revive on a long cooldown still saves dungeon runs. Keep her fast and bulky; she enables aggressive DPS picks who would otherwise die in wave 2.

Skeleton Knight (Rare STR)

Armored frontliner who protects allies and applies debuffs. Excellent if your reroll lacked control — he bridges tank and utility until you summon epics.

Fara / Gozu (Rare damage)

Strong rerolls only if you already have a plan for survivability. Fara debuffs; Gozu executes low targets. Rush a healer or tank from summons before pushing hard content.

Early game checklist

  • Accept your reroll once you hit Etherstone Golem or a role-filling Rare you like
  • Level your starter to max before spreading resources thin across six units
  • Farm basic artifact sets from early dungeons — Speed and HP% matter more than perfect sets
  • Don't chase champion duplicates until your core four are geared
  • Save premium summon currency for rate-up banners once you know your team's weak points

Summary

Rerolling is about landing a champion who can anchor your first competitive team — whether that's a Mythic like Etherstone Golem or a reliable Rare like Ursan. Stop when you get a keeper, build four roles around them, and invest gear before you chase more summons.
